BEFORE WE MAKE IT

A few useful answers.

What should I send for a print enquiry?+

Share your model file if you have one, the intended use, approximate dimensions, quantity, and preferred color. If you are starting with an idea, describe it and we can discuss the next step.

How much will a print cost?+

A quote depends on the model, material, size, print time, and finishing requirements. Get in touch with your details for a project-specific discussion.

Can I ask about a prototype or a custom part?+

Yes. Tell me what the part needs to do, any fit requirements, and your timeline. We can discuss whether 3D printing is the right approach.
